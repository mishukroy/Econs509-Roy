For each test, record its ID, input, measured value, acceptance criterion, and pass/fail/pending status. If a test fails, report the state or file responsible and stop the affected work. A criterion cannot be weakened, nor can an early failure be postponed to a later stage. Changes require my approval, a new commit, a dated explanation, and rerunning the affected checks. 1. **Reproduce the manual kernel.** Run plain VFI on $N=100$ uniform points on $[0,20]$, starting from zero and using $\tau=10^{-10}$. Compared with `manual/kernel_output.npz`, the values must satisfy $\max|V_{agent}-V_{manual}|\leq10^{-8}$ and the zero-based `G` entries must agree exactly. Report the largest value difference, policy mismatch count, and both outer iteration counts. My manual reference must remain unchanged and cannot be generated by the implementation agent.

2. **Savings policy is nondecreasing in assets.** For each shock on an ascending asset grid, check `G[n+1,s] >= G[n,s]` at every adjacent pair. Report the number and locations of any violations. The computed policy must not be rearranged to satisfy this check.

3. **Consumption is strictly positive at the chosen policy.** Policy indices must be integers in `[0,N-1]`. Recalculate consumption from the budget using the saved assets and prices. All entries must be finite and strictly positive, and the saved consumption must agree within absolute $10^{-12}$. Report the minimum consumption and its state, together with any invalid indices or budget discrepancies. Consumption must not be clipped to pass this check.

4. **The invariant distribution sums to one.** Each reported invariant distribution must have finite entries and satisfy $|\sum_m\pi_m-1|\leq10^{-10}$. Report the sum and its deviation from one.

5. **The invariant distribution is nonnegative.** Before any allowed roundoff correction, the normalized vector must have minimum at least $-10^{-14}$. The saved probabilities must be nonnegative. Report the raw minimum and the correction mass. Any correction follows §2.4 of `spec.md`, after which tests 4 and 8 must pass again.

6. **The accepted grid has no mass on the top node.** On validation and accepted grids, require $p_N=\sum_s\pi_{N,s}\leq10^{-12}$. Report $N$, $k_{max}$, and the top-node mass. Use the same threshold to assess trial ranges, keeping their probability mass and original bounds in the reported results.

7. **Model inputs and the Bellman calculation.** Check the parameters, shock order, prices, and grid formula against `spec.md`. Integer and discrete settings must match exactly; numerical definitions must agree within absolute $10^{-12}$. On the validation grid, use `V[n,s]=(n/(N-1))**2+0.3*s` with zero-based indices, and calculate a Bellman step independently by looping over every feasible savings choice and shock. The vectorized and scalar values must agree within $10^{-12}\max(1,\|T(V)\|_\infty)$, with the same policy under the smallest-index tie rule. Report the largest discrepancy. Repeat this comparison using `k=[0,0.5,1,2]`, `P=[[0.8,0.2],[0.3,0.7]]`, the same value formula, and fixed PS1 prices. This separate test case checks the orientation of shock probabilities; the economic model is unchanged.

8. **Policy-induced transition and stationarity.** Check `Q.shape == (2*N,2*N)`, nonnegative entries, and maximum row-sum error $\leq10^{-12}$. Each row must send assets to the chosen policy index, with next-shock probabilities equal to its row of `P`. In representation comparisons, dense and CSR entries must agree within $10^{-12}$. Every computed distribution must satisfy $\|Q^T\pi-\pi\|_\infty\leq10^{-10}$, with shock marginals within $10^{-10}$ of $(0.5,0.5)$. Report any incorrect transitions, row-sum errors, the stationarity residual, and shock marginals.

9. **Agreement across distribution methods and consistency of experiments.** The six distributions based on the same Howard policy must first pass tests 4, 5, and 8. Their maximum pairwise sup-norm difference must then be $\leq10^{-8}$; report the pair with the largest difference. From part (e) onward, use the recorded method choices. In part (g), keep the methods, upper bound, and grid formula fixed. Report any departures from these settings.

10. **Stopping criterion and iteration counts.** Recompute $d(x)$ from each saved household solution.
